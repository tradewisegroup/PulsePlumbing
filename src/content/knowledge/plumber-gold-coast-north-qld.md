## Why Northern Gold Coast Properties Need a Local Plumber

The northern Gold Coast — particularly Pimpama, Coomera, and Upper Coomera — is one of Australia's fastest-growing residential corridors. This rapid growth creates specific plumbing challenges:

**New estate developments (Pimpama, Coomera, Upper Coomera):**
- PE100 water mains and PVC sewer reticulation installed to City of Gold Coast and QUU standards
- Ongoing slab movement and soil settlement can affect drainage gradients in the first 5–10 years
- Water hammer and pressure issues are common as new subdivisions come online and pressure zones are established
- Multiple stages of development mean construction-related pipe damage occurs

**Established areas (Helensvale, Oxenford, Southport):**
- Older clay and PVC drainage systems with root intrusion
- Hot water systems reaching end of life
- Commercial plumbing maintenance for established retail and commercial precincts

## Gold Coast Water Authority and Council

- **City of Gold Coast** — development approval and local government standards for operational plumbing works
- **Queensland Urban Utilities (QUU)** — manages water and sewer infrastructure across parts of the northern Gold Coast
- **Gold Coast City Council Water (trading as City of Gold Coast)** — manages water supply and sewerage for the Gold Coast LGA

All plumbing work in the Gold Coast area must comply with the **Plumbing and Drainage Act 2018 (Qld)** and the **Standard Plumbing and Drainage Regulation 2019**. Form 9 compliance certificates are issued by Pulse on completion of all notifiable work.
