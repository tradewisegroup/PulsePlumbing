## Why Logan City Has Distinct Plumbing Challenges

Logan City's housing stock spans a wide age range — from 1960s–1980s fibrous cement and terracotta homes in established suburbs to modern PVC-and-PE developments in growth corridors. This creates plumbing challenges that require experience across multiple eras of construction.

**Established Logan suburbs (Springwood, Browns Plains, Woodridge, Kingston):**
- Ageing earthenware (terracotta) drain systems with significant root intrusion in areas with established tree canopy
- Galvanised steel water supply pipes in older homes — corroding and restricting flow
- Hot water systems installed 15–20+ years ago reaching end of life
- Block drain issues in older stormwater infrastructure

**Growth corridor areas (Flagstone, Jimboomba, Park Ridge):**
- New PVC and PE infrastructure in residential estates
- Active civil construction creating ground movement and pipe damage risk
- Infrastructure connections to Logan City Council and Queensland Urban Utilities networks

## Logan City Water Authority
