## Plumbing Characteristics of Ipswich Properties

**Older Ipswich suburbs (Booval, Bundamba, Ipswich CBD, Brassall):**
Properties built before 1980 typically have terracotta (earthenware) drainage systems. These are prone to tree root intrusion, cracking, and joint separation. CCTV inspection every 2–3 years is recommended. Copper hot water and supply pipes are common and may require replacing in older homes.

**Growth corridor suburbs (Springfield, Ripley, Augustine Heights):**
These areas have modern PVC drainage and PE water supply installed post-2000. The newer pipe systems are more durable but the rapid development of the area means some infrastructure was installed under time pressure — drain condition issues and settlement-related pipe movement do occur. Earthworks-related pipe damage is also more common during the active construction phase in Ripley Valley.
