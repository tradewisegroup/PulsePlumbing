## Brisbane South Plumbing Characteristics

Brisbane's south side contains some of the city's oldest housing stock, creating specific plumbing challenges:

**Inner south suburbs (Coorparoo, Greenslopes, Annerley, Holland Park):**
Properties built in the 1950s–1970s commonly have terracotta (earthenware) drainage systems that are highly susceptible to tree root intrusion given the area's mature tree canopy. Copper water supply pipes in homes of this age are approaching the end of their service life. Hot water systems in older homes frequently require replacement. Sewer connections may pass through or near established tree root zones.

**Middle south (Sunnybank, Eight Mile Plains, Mount Gravatt, Runcorn):**
A mix of 1980s–1990s PVC drainage and some remaining earthenware in older sections. Tree root issues remain common. Hot water system failures are frequent given the age of properties. Stormwater management on the relatively flat terrain in areas like Runcorn and Eight Mile Plains requires attention.

**Bayside (Wynnum, Manly, Capalaba, Alexandra Hills):**
Sandy soils in Redland-adjacent bayside suburbs can cause drain joint separation over time. Coastal properties may experience accelerated corrosion of metal components. Stormwater management on low-lying land near the bay is a consideration for some properties.
